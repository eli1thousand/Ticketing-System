<h1>Ticketing System: Password Reset</h1>

<h2>Scenario: Ms. Belle has forgotten her password to her user account and is locked out. </h2>

<h2>Procedure</h2>
<h3> First, I open a ticket with the information of the employee and their issue. A ticket consists of the organization, the ticket submitter and their email, a description of the incident and the severity rating of the ticket. </h3>
<img width="576" height="662" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca73e49f-1170-48aa-92fb-1c02344cbb91" />

<h3>Next, I will confirm their identity using multifactor authorization. This would be confirming their employee ID, submitting a ticket</h3>

<h3>With the identity of the employee being confirmed, I can now go into AD DS to reset her password.</h3>
<h3>There are other ways to do password resets, however I prefer to do it through Powershell.</h3>
