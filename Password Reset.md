<h1>Ticketing System: Password Reset</h1>

<h2>Scenario: Ms. Belle has forgotten her password to her user account and is locked out. </h2>

<h2>Procedure</h2>
<h3> First, I open a ticket with the information of the employee and their issue. A ticket consists of the organization, the ticket submitter and their email, a description of the incident and the severity rating of the ticket. </h3>
